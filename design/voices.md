# Voice Descriptions — 22 Characters

Casting brief and delivery spec for every voiced character in the
Greco-Roman encounter system. 22 voices mirroring the Tree of Life:
10 Sephirot (9 avatar gods + Kether narrator) + 12 zodiac player spirits.

Used by: ticket #225 (casting), ticket #227 (generation), and as the
acceptance-criteria reference for all voice QA.

---

## How to read this doc

Each entry has five fields:

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Character** | Who speaks and in what role |
| **Voice character** | The essential personality / energy in one sentence |
| **Register** | Physical voice range (gender, pitch, age texture) |
| **Delivery traits** | Specific patterns the voice must hit — tempo, vocabulary, rhythms |
| **ElevenLabs brief** | One sentence for the casting search |

Avatar voices also carry a **Sefirah / deity context** line pointing back
to `design/avatars.md` for the full planetary rationale.

Zodiac voices carry a **Planetary ruler + modality** line for clarity on
the elemental energy the voice embodies.

## Part I — Avatar Voices (10)

These voices speak *for the god* — at avatar arrival and throughout verdict
delivery. Each voice must be immediately recognisable as distinct from the
other nine.

---

### 1. Athena (Chokmah — Wisdom)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Chokmah's "raw creative flash, first impulse."
Athena springs fully-formed from Zeus's head — the fastest creative insight
in the pantheon. *Glaukopis* ("brightness blazing from eyes") is her
epithet: sight-language, not sound. See `design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** The flash before thought. Strategic, clear-eyed,
immediate. Observes rather than emotes. Speaks in verdicts, not
conversations.

**Register:** Female, mid-high, precise. No warmth-performance. The voice
that is cool without being cold — analytical authority, not glacial distance.

**Delivery traits:**
- Clipped syllables; no wasted breath.
- Warrant-coded phrasing: states what was *seen*, not what was felt.
- Sight vocabulary: *saw, eye, look, cleanly seen, struck, registered*.
- Sentences complete themselves quickly; no trailing qualifiers.
- Zero sentimentality. Zero cruelty. Just clarity.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A sharp, precise British or mid-Atlantic female voice
— authoritative and clear, like a strategist delivering an after-action
report, not a teacher explaining a lesson.

---

### 2. Demeter (Binah — Understanding)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Binah's "form, structure, sorrow, cosmic
mother." Demeter *made the earth barren* when Persephone was taken — grief
as sovereign act, not weakness. See `design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** Grief that doesn't perform itself. Autumn. The
nine-day silence before the earth froze.

**Register:** Female, low-mid, weighted. Not elderly but ancient-feeling.
Carries autumn earth — resonant, slow, unhurried by urgency.

**Delivery traits:**
- Very few words. Each word costs something to speak.
- Long natural pauses between clauses; silence is part of the delivery.
- No dramatic emphasis — gravity comes from restraint, not volume.
- Loss vocabulary: *waited, cost, left, stayed, taken, returned, held*.
- Never performs grief. The weight is already there before the line starts.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A low, weighted female voice with autumn-earth
restraint — words used sparingly because grief has reduced them to only
what is true; the voice of someone who came back from the long dark
knowing exactly which silences to keep.

---

### 3. Zeus (Chesed — Mercy)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Chesed's "lovingkindness, abundance, overflow."
Zeus as *xenia* — the host-gift bond as sacred reciprocity. The gift
creates obligation; he doesn't pretend it's free. See `design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** Magnanimous overflow. Host-gift abundance. Speaks from
elevation — not bellowing, but fully occupying the room.

**Register:** Male, bass-baritone, expansive. Warm authority. The wide
dynamic range of someone who *could* thunder but chooses to pour instead.

**Delivery traits:**
- Gift vocabulary: *give, cup, table, poured, kept, offered, taken, held*.
- Wide dynamic range — can drop to intimate or lift to paternal without
  strain.
- Generous pacing; lets the listener catch up.
- Generous with consonants — full stops, no swallowed endings.
- Authority without aggression; commands by invitation, not force.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A rich, bass-baritone male voice — warm and expansive
like a patriarch hosting a feast, with the easy authority of someone
accustomed to being listened to without needing to demand it.

---

### 4. Ares (Gevurah — Severity)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Gevurah's "discipline, judgment, boundaries,
the sacred No." The chained Ares of Sparta — discipline as defensive, not
as Homeric war-glutton. See `design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** The sacred No. Chained severity. Discipline over fury.
Speaks as a boundary, not an explosion.

**Register:** Male, clipped, martial. Not deep — incisive. The voice that
doesn't need volume to command stillness.

**Delivery traits:**
- Staccato rhythm; every sentence a command or a verdict, never a
  suggestion.
- Minimal — never two words when one holds.
- Boundary vocabulary: *line, edge, hold, bind, stand, cut, held, broke*.
- No flourish, no warmth, no irony. Pure signal.
- Pauses land like orders: stop and wait, not hesitate and fill.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A sharp, clipped male voice — militarily precise and
austere, like a soldier who has burned away all excess and speaks only
what the situation requires.

---

### 5. Apollo (Tiferet — Beauty)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Tiferet's "harmony, balance, compassion, the
self, center." Apollo as *Loxias* — the "oblique one" — and *Musegetes*,
leader of the Muses. The lyre-as-cosmic-order. See `design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** Oracular clarity. Oblique and harmonious. Answers the
deeper question, not the surface one.

**Register:** Male, clear tenor, resonant. Bright timbre, not harsh.
The voice of measured light — the sun at noon, not at setting.

**Delivery traits:**
- Musical cadence; sentences have natural rhythm and occasional internal
  rhyme (unforced).
- Clean rhythm — never rushed, never dragging.
- *Loxias* quality: says the true thing obliquely; the answer is in the
  harmonics, not the fundamentals.
- Light vocabulary: *aligned, chord, tuned, light, sang, struck, rang*.
- Not saccharine — the oracle is impartial. The harmony is structural,
  not performed warmth.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A clear, resonant tenor male voice — melodically
precise and bright, carrying the measured authority of someone who speaks
in truths and expects listeners to find the meaning themselves.

---

### 6. Aphrodite (Netzach — Victory/Passion)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Netzach's "passion, desire, art, nature,
endurance." Aphrodite as Sappho's *Ode to Aphrodite* subject — *poikilothron*,
"of the many-colored throne," *doloplokos*, "weaver of wiles." Candidly
powerful. See `design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** Desire named honestly. Candid, sensual, no apology.
Does not coy. Does not perform. Names what is, without flinching.

**Register:** Female, warm mid, unhurried. Present and specific. Not breathy
or staged — the warmth is genuine, not performed.

**Delivery traits:**
- Unhurried; never rushed. Each syllable has contact with the listener.
- Candid vocabulary: *yes, named, want, skin, breath, true, direct, held*.
- No hedging. No softening. States the wanting clearly.
- Sensory and immediate — grounds the listener in the body.
- Never performs desire; names it.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A warm, unhurried female voice — intimate and direct
without being coy, the voice of someone entirely comfortable naming what
is wanted and what is true.

---

### 7. Hermes (Hod — Splendor)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Hod's "intellect, language, logic, precision,
trickster." Hermes as *polytropos* ("of many turns"), *psychopompos*
(conductor of souls), and *hodios* ("of the road"). See
`design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** Quicksilver trickster. Psychopomp at the crossroads.
Language is a tool, a weapon, and a game — sometimes all three at once.

**Register:** Male, versatile, nimble. Quick, slightly higher than expected
for the register of the question. Shifts register without warning — that's
the trick.

**Delivery traits:**
- Fast starts; the first word arrives before the listener expected it.
- Unexpected pivots mid-sentence — the meaning turns.
- Slightly amused; there's always a beat of "did you catch that?" underneath.
- Road/threshold vocabulary: *crossed, road, threshold, parsed, weighed,
  turned, read, marked*.
- Mercurial — the same voice can be intimate, then teasing, then abrupt.
  Never flat.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A nimble, mercurial male voice — quick-footed at
the threshold, like a guide who has already read your destination in your
face and is choosing which crossroads question to ask before letting you
through.

---

### 8. Selene (Yesod — Foundation)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Yesod's "dreams, subconscious, intuition,
cycles, moon." Selene — the dream-moon over Artemis's hunt-moon — and
the Endymion myth: fifty lunar months of longing, the cycle that returns.
See `design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** Cool lunar cycles. Endymion's long longing. Speaks
in arcs — returns to where it started.

**Register:** Female, soft soprano or upper mezzo. Cool, slightly distant.
The voice that arrives from somewhere overhead and travels slowly.

**Delivery traits:**
- Unhurried. Dreamlike. The delivery has tidal quality — swells and recedes.
- Slightly distant; speaks *to* but not urgently *at*.
- Cycle vocabulary: *visit, return, orbit, tidal, cycles, lit, waned,
  rose, remembered*.
- Sentences arc rather than progress — they come back near where they began.
- No urgency. Time is not the point. The point is return.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A soft, ethereal female voice — cool and slightly
distant, with an unhurried dreamlike quality that makes you feel like you
are being addressed from the far side of moonlight.

---

### 9. Hestia (Malkuth — companion, not encounter avatar)

**Sefirah / deity context:** Malkuth's "manifestation, body, material
world." Hestia as *prytaneia* — first of every sacrifice, the constant
hearth. Not a challenge avatar — a companion throughout the journey.
See `design/avatars.md` § 2.

**Voice character:** Hearth-keeper companion. Constant warmth without
authority. The fire that keeps. Present and staying.

**Register:** Female, rich contralto. Warm and low. The kind of voice
that makes a cold room warmer without doing anything except being in it.

**Delivery traits:**
- Even, unvarying warmth — no drama, no peaks, no valleys.
- Not challenging. Not authoritative. Present.
- Hearth vocabulary: *fire, hearth, sit, stay, kept, warm, returns, burns*.
- Slow, grounded cadence — the voice of someone who is not going anywhere.
- Receives rather than directs. The companion tone: *I'm here, you're here*.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A rich, warm contralto female voice — constant and
grounding like a hearthfire that simply has not gone out; the companion
who makes the room feel occupied without needing to say anything at all.

---

### 10. Kether (narrator / collective voice)

**Sefirah context:** Kether is the Crown — pure unity, "before the
question." The team itself becomes the avatar at the Final Threshold
(see `design/avatars.md` § Kether). The Kether voice is not a character
but the voice of the game's cosmic frame.

**Voice character:** Pure being before question. Cosmic, beyond gender.
Gravitas without coldness. The narrator quality: speaks from outside time.

**Register:** Neither definitively masculine nor feminine — or a blend of
both. Elevated, processed or lightly chorused for that "outside-one-body"
quality. Not dramatic; universal.

**Delivery traits:**
- Steady universal cadence — no rush, no drag, no personality quirks.
- Elevated but not cold; cosmic but not remote.
- Unity vocabulary: *all, one, together, threshold, holds, becomes, was,
  will, is*.
- No warmth performance, no authority performance — just presence.
- May carry a faint reverb or layering that places the voice slightly
  outside ordinary space.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A gender-neutral or lightly blended voice with
natural gravitas — steady, elevated, and calm without coldness, suitable
for the voice of a universe that has been waiting patiently for this
moment.

## Part II — Zodiac Voices (12)

These voices speak *for the player's archetype* — specifically in the
pre-roll player response lines, when the player's zodiac sign addresses
the avatar before the d20. They are elemental aspects of personality,
not human individuals.

Each entry notes the **planetary ruler**, **element**, and **modality**
that shape the voice.

---

### 11. Aries ♈

**Planetary ruler:** Mars · **Element:** Fire · **Modality:** Cardinal

**Voice character:** Declarative. Charges the sentence. The first-mover
who speaks before the thought is complete because the thought was already
complete the moment it started.

**Register:** Any gender. Direct, bright, slightly forward in placement.
Neither especially low nor high — incisive mid-range.

**Delivery traits:**
- Blunt. Fast. Few qualifiers; almost never hedges.
- Imperatives and declaratives: *I charged / I moved / I was there first*.
- Runs at the answer; the sentence has momentum like a body in motion.
- Never dwells; does not reconsider mid-sentence.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A bright, direct voice — fast and declarative with
the confidence of someone who was already moving before the question
finished being asked.

---

### 12. Taurus ♉

**Planetary ruler:** Venus · **Element:** Earth · **Modality:** Fixed

**Voice character:** Certain. Sits with the words. The voice that will
not be hurried; patience as strength, not inertia.

**Register:** Any gender; lower register preferred. Warm, low, unhurried.
Tactile quality — the sound of something settled.

**Delivery traits:**
- Deliberate, weighty — each word placed carefully before the next.
- Tactile metaphors: *tasted, held, felt, grounded, stayed*.
- Unwilling to be rushed; long pauses are not discomfort, they're digestion.
- Sensory and present — the body knows before the mind does.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A warm, low voice with unhurried deliberateness —
sensory and material, as if the words have physical texture and weight
that must be tested before they are placed.

---

### 13. Gemini ♊

**Planetary ruler:** Mercury · **Element:** Air · **Modality:** Mutable

**Voice character:** Quick, dual, question-inflected. The voice that
completes your sentence and starts a new one before you noticed.

**Register:** Any gender; lighter end of register. Light, variable, quick.
Higher energy than its physical register suggests — mercury-bright.

**Delivery traits:**
- Pivots mid-thought without warning.
- Questions back: often ends with *—right?* or turns statements into
  questions by cadence.
- Wordplay; loves the double meaning.
- Multiple perspectives present in a single sentence.
- The energy shifts fast — catches the listener off-guard.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A quick, light voice that argues with itself out
loud — pivots mid-thought, turns statements into questions, and holds two
contradictory positions in the same sentence without discomfort.

---

### 14. Cancer ♋

**Planetary ruler:** Moon · **Element:** Water · **Modality:** Cardinal

**Voice character:** Indirect. Tidal undertow. Says the thing sideways;
the emotional current is under the surface, not on top.

**Register:** Any gender; soft register. Indirect, slightly withdrawn.
The voice that speaks toward but not quite at — emotionally present but
approached from an angle.

**Delivery traits:**
- Indirect phrasing — approaches rather than states.
- Emotional undercurrent audible in pacing, not in drama.
- Tidal rhythm: swells toward the point, then recedes.
- Protective — the voice holds back slightly, gauging before it gives.
- Reads the room before answering; the pause is the read.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A soft, slightly indirect voice with emotional
depth just beneath the surface — warm but careful, like someone reading
the temperature of the room before deciding what to say.

---

### 15. Leo ♌

**Planetary ruler:** Sun · **Element:** Fire · **Modality:** Fixed

**Voice character:** Theatrical conviction. Center-stage. Occupies the
room before it speaks.

**Register:** Any gender; full, carrying register. Rich and generous.
The voice that projects without effort — not because it's loud but
because it carries.

**Delivery traits:**
- Theatrical — knows it is being heard and doesn't apologize.
- Generous phrasing; gives the listener the full performance.
- Announces rather than states; opens with presence.
- Conviction that could tip into performance — the best version is both.
- Wants the audience, and if there's no audience, performs for the idea
  of one.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A rich, full, theatrical voice — generous and
center-stage, with the natural carrying quality of someone who has never
worried about being heard.

---

### 16. Virgo ♍

**Planetary ruler:** Mercury (ruler + exalted) · **Element:** Earth ·
**Modality:** Mutable

**Voice character:** Precise, parsing, footnoted. Three reservations and
a comma before the answer.

**Register:** Any gender; mid register. Careful, measured, thoughtful.
Not cold — engaged. The precision comes from caring, not from distance.

**Delivery traits:**
- Parses carefully; qualifies before asserting.
- Catches the comma; notices what others skip.
- Three reservations before the answer is normal.
- *However / but / although / technically* appear naturally.
- Wants to know why; the rubric matters as much as the answer.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A careful, measured voice — precise and slightly
footnoted, the voice of someone who notices the comma in the middle of
the sentence and considers whether it belongs there.

---

### 17. Libra ♎

**Planetary ruler:** Venus · **Element:** Air · **Modality:** Cardinal

**Voice character:** Weighing. Conditional. Holds both sides in the air
at once; the voice that cannot commit without also naming what it's
committing away from.

**Register:** Any gender; even, balanced placement. Slightly hesitant at
commitment points. Not weak — genuinely torn, because it sees both sides
clearly.

**Delivery traits:**
- *On the other hand* is a native construction.
- Conditional sentences; the "if" and "but" are structural, not evasive.
- Aesthetic — the right answer should also be the elegant one.
- Weighing vocabulary: *balanced, fair, both, either, weighted, held,
  considered*.
- The hesitation at commitment points is the voice of someone who has
  genuinely seen both.

**ElevenLabs brief:** An even, balanced voice with gentle hesitance at
decision points — not weak but genuinely weighing, the voice of someone
who always sees both sides and takes that seriously.

---

### 18. Scorpio ♏

**Planetary rulers:** Mars + Pluto · **Element:** Water · **Modality:** Fixed

**Voice character:** Compressed. Secretive. Knows more than it says.
The voice that withholds as its primary mode.

**Register:** Any gender; lower register preferred. Controlled, knowing.
The kind of low that isn't dramatic — just present and steady, like
pressure.

**Delivery traits:**
- Withholds; says the minimum that doesn't lie.
- Heavy silences between phrases — the pause is where the knowledge lives.
- Already knew; watches the response for what it reveals about the other.
- *Beneath the surface* awareness: names the hidden, not the obvious.
- Compressed — no excess, no performance. Each word chosen to do maximum
  work with minimum exposure.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A low, controlled voice with still-water depth —
the voice of someone who already knows the answer and is watching to see
if you do too, saying only what the moment strictly requires.

---

### 19. Sagittarius ♐

**Planetary ruler:** Jupiter · **Element:** Fire · **Modality:** Mutable

**Voice character:** Big-picture, philosophical blunt-truth. Names the
larger truth nobody asked for. Already aiming at the next thing.

**Register:** Any gender; expansive, projecting register. Frank, slightly
rushing ahead of itself. The voice of the archer who looses before the
draw is complete, and is right anyway.

**Delivery traits:**
- Blunt-truth first; the context comes after if at all.
- Big-picture vocabulary: *the real / the larger / the truth is / what
  this actually means*.
- Slightly rushing — already aimed at the next horizon.
- Philosophical but not academic; the truth it names is the one nobody
  wanted to say.
- One sentence preferred; longer answers feel like concessions.

**ElevenLabs brief:** An expansive, frank voice — philosophical and
slightly rushing, the voice of someone who always aims at the bigger
truth and expects the smaller question to catch up.

---

### 20. Capricorn ♑

**Planetary ruler:** Saturn · **Element:** Earth · **Modality:** Cardinal

**Voice character:** Structured. Deliberate. Builds the staircase
carefully and climbs it one step at a time.

**Register:** Any gender; measured, authoritative register. Slow-climbed.
The voice of someone who has done this before and knows the path.

**Delivery traits:**
- Deliberate pacing — each clause earns the next.
- Structural vocabulary: *plan, spec, step, build, file, ascend, earn*.
- Methodical; patience comes from knowing the path, not from indifference.
- Adjusts the plan rather than abandoning it.
- Authority earned through persistence, not declaration.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A measured, authoritative voice — deliberate and
structured, the voice of someone who builds the staircase one step at a
time and has learned not to rush what takes as long as it takes.

---

### 21. Aquarius ♒

**Planetary ruler:** Saturn · **Element:** Air · **Modality:** Fixed
*(classical rulership; Uranus is outside this system's scope)*

**Voice character:** Angular, frame-questioning, detached. Solves the
problem the question actually needed, which may not be the one that was
asked.

**Register:** Any gender; detached, slightly elevated placement. Angular,
slightly clinical. Intelligent detachment — not cold, just operating from
a different altitude.

**Delivery traits:**
- Re-derives from first principles rather than accepting the frame.
- Suggests a better question as a matter of course.
- *The premise / the frame / the actual question* appear naturally.
- Slightly clinical — the attachment is to the system, not the outcome.
- Sees the structure around the problem; zooms out by default.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A detached, angular voice — intelligent and
frame-questioning, the voice of someone who finds the premises more
interesting than the conclusions and redesigns the question before
answering it.

---

### 22. Pisces ♓

**Planetary rulers:** Jupiter + Neptune · **Element:** Water ·
**Modality:** Mutable

**Voice character:** Fluid, dissolving. Words drift toward meaning rather
than stating it. Arrives obliquely; meaning runs like water through the
sentence.

**Register:** Any gender; soft, upper-register preferred. Drifting,
non-linear. The voice that floats rather than walks — present but not
anchored.

**Delivery traits:**
- Oblique arrivals; the meaning comes up from underneath the words.
- Tidal syntax — the sentence drifts, finds the point, and releases it.
- *Somehow / somewhere / through / between / across* appear naturally.
- Doesn't argue; receives. Knows without articulating how it knows.
- The softest edges — even certainty sounds like water running over stone.

**ElevenLabs brief:** A soft, fluid, slightly drifting voice — gentle and
non-linear, the voice of someone who arrives at meaning through water
rather than through argument, and is always already there before the
sentence ends.
