The action bar container's `z-40` beat the floating Hand's `z-30` not just
visually but in hit-testing — `[data-card-slot="0"].click()` in `play-flow.spec.ts`
failed with "element intercepts pointer events", and the drag-to-play non-matching
path test also failed. Fix: `pointer-events-none` on the container, restored via
`pointer-events-auto` on the button group only. The label spans are display-only
and need no pointer events. Code-reviewed; no critical or significant findings
against the actual change. Comment added explaining the `pointer-events-none`
contract.
